## 2. Step 0 — marginal-cohort VALUE measurement (recommended first action)

**Why first.** Phase 4 established the *mechanics* (exposure 17.16 %, 505 rejections for "budget
insufficient for 1 share", multiplier never > 0.5) and that the benchmark gap is exposure rather than
selection. It did **not** establish that the marginal setups a larger budget would admit are worth
taking — and D3's BULL result (avgR −0.321) warns they may not be. Changing a parameter before
measuring this would be guessing.

**Method (read-only, deterministic, no re-simulation of the strategy):**
for each of the 505 budget-rejected setups, take the cached OHLCV bars forward from its signal date
and compute a **fixed-horizon forward outcome** in R units using the same stop/target geometry the
system already applies (stop distance = ATR × 1.5, target = ATR × 2.5, both from the recorded
`atr`), reporting: hit-target / hit-stop / neither, forward R at the horizon, and MFE/MAE.
Group by (rejection reason × regime label at signal × price band).

**Decision rule (pre-registered):**

| Step-0 outcome | Consequence |
|---|---|
| marginal cohort expectancy **≥** accepted cohort (currently +0.182 R) | the deployment lever is worth a controlled experiment → go to §3 |
| marginal cohort expectancy **> 0 but <** accepted cohort | the lever helps return but dilutes per-trade quality → prefer the *capital-base* lever (#6) over loosening the multiplier |
| marginal cohort expectancy **≤ 0** | the cap is protective; **do not** touch multipliers → target moves to Exit (#3) or to making #4 observable |
| cohorts too small / contradictory across halves | report as inconclusive; do not proceed on this lever |

**Cost:** one pass over the cached bars (~1 minute). **Files touched:** a new read-only script +
one report. **No parameter, no config, no production path, no backtest re-run.**

## 4. Guardrails (pre-registered, apply to every experiment)

1. **One variable at a time**, and the change is stated before the run.
2. **Risk-adjusted success only** — the criteria are Sharpe and MaxDD together; raw return alone can
   never be the deciding metric (this window is a bull market, so more exposure mechanically looks
   better regardless of skill).
3. **Out-of-window confirmation required** before any promotion: the same test must be repeated on
   2018–2025 (cache covers it) and must not reverse.
4. **No production wiring** and no behaviour change to the live path during Phase 5; `exit_engine_mode`
   stays `legacy`.
5. **Every experiment reports its failures**, including the ones that contradict the hypothesis, in
   the same report as the successes.
6. **Freeze contracts are not edited** — a lever that needs one gets an architecture decision *first*,
   and the decision is recorded in the registry.
