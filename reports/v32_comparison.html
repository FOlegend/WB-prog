v3.2 Controlled Repair Experiments — Regime as Throttle, Not Door

Period 2018-01-01 ~ 2025-07-31 - Monthly rebalance - Top 20 - Same dynamic universe as graduation exam (frozen parameters, no mid-run tuning).

Core hypothesis (reviewer): "Regime engine defends but doesn't attack." The problem is NOT that regime_score is wrong - it's that the exposure response is too extreme. When score < 50, the system goes completely to cash and stays there too long, missing recoveries. v3.2 tests whether making the exposure response continuous (throttle) instead of binary (door) fixes this.
